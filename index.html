Key Takeaways

  • The Budget Gaming Rig is the most realistic option for keeping costs under $900 while still handling modern games smoothly.
  • Stepping up to the Mid-Range Creator setup doubles the RAM and provides a solid graphics boost for $450 more.
  • The Top-Tier Workstation costs nearly triple the budget build, making it unnecessary unless you are doing heavy video editing or intensive rendering.
